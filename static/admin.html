Refinement steps

Every picture of the last refinements, side by side.

Brush up is the picture on the iPad when your child lifts the brush. Then come the refinement steps: the pictures sent while the drawing pauses.

Change is how much a step differs from the step before, drift how far it has moved from "brush up": the mean difference (0 to 255) at 1/16 size, so only layout and big colour areas count (as in tools/refine_lab.py). A step over 6 change or 6 drift is marked red. Times are after the pause.

The last 5 runs are kept in memory only. Save batch writes one to data/refine-batches/ with the drawing and all settings, so it can be replayed with tools/replay_batch.py.
